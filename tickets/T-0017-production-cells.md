# Add production cells and seven resources

The city must feed the roster through explicit cells and routes. A global menu is not enough.

## Checks

- [ ] Seven resource classes with mass and energy conservation.
- [ ] One production cell per district, with local buffers.
- [ ] Power, range and permissions can cut networked stock.
- [ ] Local, scheduled and networked logistics share one status model.
- [ ] Faults name starved, blocked, unpowered and damaged states.
- [ ] A dashboard shows each machine's fault history.
- [ ] 24 named residents with food, rest, morale and skills.
- [ ] Species and settlement factions can both exist.
- [ ] A downed resident is recoverable or permanent.

## Exit

A blocked cell names its cause, and a power cut cuts the network.
